Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 August 2026

Information we collect

We collect contact details, appointment preferences, payment references, consent records, and medical intake information you choose to provide. Payment card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, and bank credentials are processed by Stripe or Razorpay and are not stored by this website.

How information is used

Information is used to schedule and provide dental care, process booking payments and refunds, send confirmations and reminders, manage cancellations and waitlists, prevent duplicate bookings, and meet legal or operational requirements.

Storage and access

Booking data is kept in protected Firebase services and private cloud archives. Medical intake data is accessible only to authorized clinic administrators. Administrative actions are logged.

Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate bookings, payments, email, and cloud hosting, or where required by law. We do not sell patient information.

Retention and choices

Contact the clinic to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Clinical, financial, fraud-prevention, or legal records may need to be retained. Cancellation and refund rights are described in the booking terms.

Security

We use gateway signature verification, restricted database rules, administrator authentication, rate limits, private archives, and audit records. No internet system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the details on the Contact page. The clinic must replace placeholder contact details before launch.
