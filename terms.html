Appointment and Payment Terms

Effective: 16 August 2026

Appointment confirmation

A selected time is temporarily held during payment. An appointment is confirmed only after the payment gateway verifies successful payment and a confirmation is issued. A treatment selection is for scheduling and does not guarantee a diagnosis, final treatment plan, or final treatment price.

Booking fee

The amount displayed by the payment gateway is a booking or consultation fee. Any additional treatment cost will be explained by the clinic after assessment.

Cancellation and refunds

Patient cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled visit may be submitted for a refund to the original payment method. Gateway processing times apply. Late cancellations and no-shows may not be refundable. The clinic may issue exceptions where appropriate.

Rescheduling

Confirmed bookings may be rescheduled to an available time. Repeated changes, emergencies, clinical requirements, or clinic closures may require staff assistance.

Late or conflicting payment

Technical delays are checked before a slot is confirmed. If a payment arrives after a reservation has expired and the time is no longer available, the payment is placed under manual review and will not create a duplicate appointment.

Emergencies

Online booking is not an emergency service. Seek appropriate emergency medical care when urgent symptoms require it.

Consent

By booking, you consent to necessary appointment emails and agree to the Privacy Policy. Medical intake information should be accurate to the best of your knowledge.
